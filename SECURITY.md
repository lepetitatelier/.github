# Security

Most of our repos are hardware: plans, printed parts, machines. A "security problem" here is usually one of:

- **a safety problem**: a design or instruction that could burn, shock, cut or poison someone. Open an issue in that
  project and say so in the first line: safety reports are looked at first. If someone is in danger right now, stop
  using the machine and unplug it.
- **a software problem**: a vulnerability in our code, the website (lepetitatelier.nz) or its forms, or a secret
  committed by mistake. **Don't open a public issue.** Use **Report a vulnerability** on the repo's Security tab, or
  write to bonjour@lepetitatelier.nz with "security" in the subject.

We're a small workshop: we reply within a week, and fix what we can as fast as we can. Thank you for telling us.
