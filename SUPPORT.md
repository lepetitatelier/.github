# Getting help

- **A question about a project:** open an issue in its repo and choose **A question**. All projects:
  [lepetitatelier.nz/projects](https://lepetitatelier.nz/projects/).
- **You built one:** open an issue and choose **I built this**. We love these.
- **Workshops, visits, donating plastic or tools:** [lepetitatelier.nz/get-involved](https://lepetitatelier.nz/get-involved/#contact)
  or bonjour@lepetitatelier.nz.

We're a small workshop in Akaroa, New Zealand: a person reads everything, usually within a week.
