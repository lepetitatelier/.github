# How we behave

Le Petit Atelier is an open workshop: anyone can walk in, and anyone can take part here. That only works if everyone
feels welcome, in the garage and online.

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with every level of experience. There are no stupid questions.
- **Be generous with credit.** Say whose design you built on. Link it.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** Show the photo, the measurement, the test.
- **Respect privacy.** No photos of people without their consent. No personal details of others.
- **Safety first.** Don't encourage anyone to skip a guard, a fume extractor or a fuse.

Not OK, anywhere: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, threats, or publishing someone's private
information.

If something isn't right, write to bonjour@lepetitatelier.nz. We read it in confidence and act on it: a word, a
warning, or removing someone from the project. Sebastien, who runs Le Petit Atelier, decides; if the complaint is about
Sebastien, say so and it will be handled by someone outside the project.
